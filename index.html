📜 Rules & Gameplay

1. Setup & Starting

4 Groups set up in the class. Each group nominates 1 active player holding 10 fingers up on display.

2. Prompts & Finger Drops

Read prompts off the main TV board. If the representative or group members have done the action, put 1 finger down!

3. The Revival Phase (0 Fingers)

When a team runs out of fingers, they enter REVIVAL immediately and must choose one option:

  • Option A (Paper Basketball): Nominate anyone in the room to throw paper balls into the classroom bin from the back! Each goal = +1 Finger. You can cash out safely, BUT if you throw again and MISS after making goals, you lose all goals from that turn!
  • Option B (Snitch / Call Out): Accuse an opponent player of lying about a prompt! Teacher Mr. Joy renders the verdict.
    • Snitch Proven TRUE: Accusing team gets 5 fingers restored, Lying team loses 3 fingers immediately!
    • Snitch FAILS: Accusing team is OUT!

4. Winning the Game

If both revival options fail or are forfeited, the team is eliminated. The last remaining group wins!
